################### 8.Marquee Text (auto scroll) #######################

✅✅Install :
	-go to: https://www.npmjs.com/package/react-marquee-text
	-copy : npm i react-marquee-text
	-paste to : vscode terminal


✅✅Use :
	-import this two :
		import MarqueeText from "react-marquee-text"
		import "MarqueeText/styles.css"
	
	-modify the last one like this:
		import "react-marquee-text/dist/styles.css"

	-Call the Marquee like this :
 		<MarqueeText>
      			//write your text here
    		</MarqueeText>

	-change direction and time:
		<MarqueeText direction="right" duration={10}>
      			Let's bring it on back to days of yore
    		</MarqueeText>
